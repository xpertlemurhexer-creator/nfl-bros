# NFL Bros

An arcade American football game that runs in the browser. Pick an NFL team, call your plays, and control one player at a time on offense and defense while the computer runs everyone else.

## Play

Download the project and double-click `index.html`. There's nothing to install.

## Modes

- **Quick Game:** any two of the 32 teams
- **Season:** 17 games and the playoffs, saved in your browser
- **Difficulty:** Easy, Normal or Hard

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Arrows or WASD | Move, or aim at a receiver as QB |
| Space | Snap, throw, stiff-arm or dive. On kicks, press once for power and again for accuracy |
| Shift | Next receiver, or switch defender |
| 1-9, 0 | Quick-pick a play |
| Esc or P | Pause |
| M | Mute |

## Team ratings

Ratings in `js/teams.js` combine 2026 records through Week 8, preseason rankings, and published team strengths and weaknesses. QB ratings are judgement calls. Update the records there to keep teams current.

## Tests

Open `tests/tests.html` from a local web server. `serve.ps1` runs one at http://localhost:8123 on Windows.
